Credisim: the true cost of a loan, in one view

A neutral, bilingual (EN/FR) web simulator for mortgages and other loans. It covers insurance, fees, the guarantee, notary costs, the TAEG and the legal limits, with charts and scenario comparison. No sign-up and no sales call.

22 September 2026Research on 12 existing toolsPrototype: docs/planning/prototype/index.html

Summary

The French market is served by broker simulators (Meilleurtaux, Pretto, Empruntis) whose job is to generate leads, and by the public ANIL tools, which are neutral but dated. Each question (payment, capacity, notary, PTZ, TAEG) lives on a separate page. German broker tools (Interhyp, Hypofriend) are better at comparing scenarios but stop at the border.

Credisim's position: one project view that stays neutral (the full budget, every loan in it, every cost). It explains the TAEG and checks it live against the usury rate and the HCSF rules, in English and French. Everything is calculated in the browser.

Gaps to exploit

These are the differentiators. They are marked gap in the feature list.

  • One neutral project viewBudget, all loans and all costs on one screen. No sign-up, no call-back.
  • The TAEG explainedShows how many points come from interest, insurance and fees, checked live against the usury rate.
  • Insurance done properlyBank contract on the initial capital vs another insurer on the remaining capital, coverage per borrower, and savings from switching insurer (Lemoine law).
  • Multi-loan packagesMain loan + PTZ + Action Logement, with the total payment smoothed to stay constant (lissage). Meilleurtaux's calculette leaves the PTZ out.
  • "Check my offer"Type in a real bank offer and verify its schedule and TAEG. No mainstream tool does this.
  • Scenario comparison2–4 scenarios overlaid, as German tools do and French tools rarely do.
  • English for FranceExpats and cross-border workers have no good English tool that knows French rules.
  • Europe in one toolPresets for France, Belgium, Germany, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands. Every current tool stops at its own border.

Other parameters

TopicRule / value
HCSFDebt ratio ≤ 35 % of net income, insurance included. Duration ≤ 25 yrs (27 for new-build with deferral). Banks may exceed these limits for 20 % of their loans.
PTZAvailable until 31 Dec 2027. 4 income tranches; finances 20–50 % of the cost for flats, 10–30 % for houses. Deferral / total length: T1 10/25 yrs, T2 8/20, T3 2/15, T4 0/10. 2026 income ceilings unchanged: €28,500 (zone C, 1 person) to €161,700 (zone A, 8+).
Notary feesExisting homes ≈ 7–8.5 %; transfer taxes 6.32 % in most départements (raised until Mar 2028). First-time buyers are exempt from the increase (5.81 %). New build ≈ 2–3 %.
GuaranteeCrédit Logement ≈ 0.75 % + €300; about 60–80 % of the mutual-fund (FMG) share is refunded at the end. A mortgage (hypothèque) costs ≈ 1–2 %.
InsuranceLemoine law: switch insurer at any time. No health questionnaire at ≤ €200k per borrower if repaid before age 60. Total-invalidity threshold set at 66 % since 1 Sep 2026.
TAEGEU actuarial method: solve for the monthly rate i in net amount = Σ flows / (1+i)k, then TAEG = (1+i)12 − 1.
CCD2From 20 Nov 2026: the TAEG also applies to loans under €200 and to buy-now-pay-later; overdraft fees count in the TAEG; standard pre-contract information (SECCI) readable on mobile.

Roadmap

Each phase ends with a review gate: you confirm it before the next one starts.

Phase 0done

HTML prototype. Financing plan, insurance, fees and guarantee, TAEG breakdown, usury and HCSF checks, charts, duration comparison, schedule, EN/FR, share link.

Phase 1built · in review

The real app. Vite + Svelte + TypeScript, and a calculation engine with its own tests. A credit type selector (mortgage, personal, car, works) and on/off toggles for each component (insurance, guarantee, fees, notary, works, other loans). Quick and Expert modes, charts, 2-scenario compare, EN/FR, share link, Firebase config.

Phase 2learn

How it works. Short, simple explainers in EN/FR: what a loan is, how the monthly payment is calculated, interest vs capital, what's inside the TAEG, insurance, guarantee, notary fees, usury rate, the 35 % rule, the PTZ. Plus "how this calculator works", with live mini-examples and links from every ⓘ.

Phase 3France advanced

PTZ as an optional component, combined with the main loan (lissage). Payment deferral, fixed-capital and interest-only loans, early repayment, renegotiation, insurer switch, check my offer, 4 scenarios, PDF/CSV export.

Phase 4more credit types

Revolving credit, leasing (LOA/LLD) vs loan, buy-now-pay-later cost, debt consolidation, bridge loan, rental investment, variable and capped rates.

Phase 5Europe & reach

Country presets (BE, DE, ES, IT, PT, NL), more languages, SEO pages, embed widget, analytics.
